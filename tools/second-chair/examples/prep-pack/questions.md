# Prepared answers

Sample answers written in advance. Second Chair shows these exactly as written when the question matches.

## Why are you looking for a new job?
I've taken the platform as far as a small team can. The next step for me is a place where AI changes how the whole company operates, with a bigger canvas.

Q: What's your management style?
A: I set clear outcomes and leave the how to the team. I check in on decisions, not tasks, and I write things down so people can disagree in comments before we meet.

**How do you handle conflict between people on your team?**
I get both people to write the strongest version of the other person's position. Most disagreements turn out to be about a missing fact, and we go find it together.
