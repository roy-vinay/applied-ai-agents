# Story bank

Sample prep pack for trying Second Chair. Every figure here is invented.

## Scaling the support operation

At Lumen Freight I inherited a support queue that took 41 hours to answer a ticket. I split the queue by customer tier, added an AI triage step that drafted replies for agents to approve, and set a rule that no draft went out without a human reading it. Median first response fell to 3 hours in one quarter, and we did it without adding headcount.

## A launch I would do differently

We launched the dispatch planner to every customer at once. Two large accounts hit a routing bug in the first week and one of them paused the contract. I now ship to 10 percent of accounts first, with a rollback switch the on-call engineer can flip without a deploy.

## Disagreeing with an executive

Our CEO wanted to build a custom pricing engine for one enterprise prospect. I showed that the prospect's requirements matched 80 percent of what our existing rules engine did, proposed a two week extension instead of a quarter of new build, and offered to own the risk if the deal slipped. We closed the deal on the extension.

## Building trust in an AI agent

I don't believe in a binary approve or deny for agents. At Lumen the routing agent earned autonomy step by step: first it suggested, then it acted with a citation and an explanation, and only lanes with a 98 percent acceptance rate over four weeks ran without review.
