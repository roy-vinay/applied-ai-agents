# Role prep: Head of Product, Example Corp

## Why this role
Example Corp is moving from a single product to a platform, which is the transition I led at Lumen Freight. I want a role where AI changes the operating model, not just a feature list.

## My 90 day plan
In the first 30 days I listen: twenty customer calls, every team's roadmap, and the last two quarters of churn reasons. By day 60 I publish one page on where the platform bets are. By day 90 we have one shipped improvement that customers can feel.

## Questions to ask them
- What would make the first year a clear success?
- Where does product sit when sales and engineering disagree?

## Weaknesses
I can move to a decision before quieter people have spoken. I now write the decision doc first and collect written comments before any meeting where we decide.
